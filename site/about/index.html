Keven Lefebvre / About

Hello, I'm Keven.

Software, music, and a few things in between. A little about Keven Lefebvre.

From Saguenay, Québec · French & English

Two old computers and a lot of questions

I learned to code by reading, trying things, and wanting to understand what was happening behind the button. HTML and CSS led to JavaScript, then PHP. Before long, I wanted a server of my own and a browser game to build on it.

In 2006, two old computers sat under a monitor at my parents' place. One ran a game server; the other ran Ubuntu and my small web projects, including a game called Orkïka. There was a lot to figure out. That was part of the fun.

The professional side

At 17, I started OrbiteWeb. Building custom websites and applications for businesses meant learning to look beyond the screen: what information people needed, how they worked, and how the pieces of a system fit together.

After roles at Arsenal Web and Turbulent, I joined Shopify, where I still work. Alongside that role, I work independently as k11e / Keven Lefebvre, offering consulting, development and workshops. I still enjoy building software, working through difficult questions with other people, and helping developers grow.

I live in Saguenay and work in French and English. I can work remotely worldwide; travel and the practical details are agreed for each engagement.

More of the story

I like understanding the problem behind a request, explaining the choices, and finding a solution that fits the people who will use it. If you're curious about working together, there's a page about how I can help, or you can simply write to me.

There's more to the picture

I sing and make music. I like the outdoors, too. And I make things for myself: Inkjack and Daraoké are two of them. Some ideas become software; others are better left as a thought, a question, or a song.

That's what this site is for. Professional work has a place here, but so do the other things that catch my attention. If something here resonates with you, say hello.
